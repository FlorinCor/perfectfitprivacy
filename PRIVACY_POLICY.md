# Perfect Fit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy describes how Perfect Fit (Android package `com.florin.perfectfit`), developed by Cordunianu Florin, handles information. It applies to versions of the game using the practices described below.

## Information handled by the game

Perfect Fit does not require an account. Its puzzles and saved progress work offline. The game includes Google AdMob app open advertising during app startup and banner advertising above the game area across all levels, when ads are available. Advertising and privacy-message requests use an internet connection; an unavailable ad does not prevent you from playing.

The game does not send saved puzzle records to the developer. It does not request access to your precise device location, contacts, camera, or microphone. Advertising services handle information separately as described below.

## Data stored on your device

The game stores the following in its private application storage to provide its features:

- Current level, completed levels, best move counts and completion times.
- Hint usage and completed tutorials.
- Unfinished puzzles, including object positions and puzzle state.
- Preferences such as sound, vibration, reduced motion, and hint settings.

These records are used to resume puzzles, remember progress, and apply your preferences. The developer does not receive or sell these records, and the game does not share them with advertising or analytics services.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This includes your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches, taps, and video views, performance and diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID when available. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. See [Google's SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

Google and its advertising partners process information under their respective policies. See [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for processing, retention, controls, and deletion information. Deleting this game's local data does not automatically delete information already held by those services.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to check privacy-message requirements at startup and present required choices before requesting ads. UMP retains your privacy-choice state for later launches. Where required, **Ad privacy options** in the game's Settings lets you review or change those choices. Available advertising and personalization depend on your region and choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through Android's advertising settings; the available controls depend on your Android version. Choosing not to personalize ads does not necessarily stop all advertising or all data processing needed to serve permitted ads.

## Android backup and device transfer

Perfect Fit allows Android to back up its saved progress and preferences and transfer them to another device. Depending on your device, account, and backup settings, your operating system or backup provider may store a copy outside your device. This is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by a server operated by the developer. The developer cannot access these backups. You can manage backup and restore through your device and account settings. For Google services, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Secure handling of personal and sensitive data

The game's progress and preference records are kept in Android app-private storage, which Android ordinarily restricts to this app. The game does not transmit those records to the developer or to advertising services. Android backup and device transfer can copy them as described above; the backup provider's security and access controls apply to those copies.

For advertising and privacy choices, the Google Mobile Ads and UMP SDKs communicate with Google and its partners. Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Google and its partners handle the information they receive under their own security and privacy practices. The game does not operate a developer-run account or gameplay-data server.

If you contact the developer, your message and email address are handled through the developer's email provider. Please avoid including sensitive information that is not needed to answer your request. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

Local game data remains in the application's private storage until you clear it or uninstall the game. You can delete local records through Android Settings by opening Perfect Fit's app information and choosing the option to clear storage or app data. This removes saved progress and settings. Uninstalling also removes local app data; backup copies may remain under your backup provider's retention rules and may be restored when you reinstall. Manage or delete those copies through your backup provider.

## Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include will be handled to respond to your inquiry. This correspondence is processed through the developer's email provider and retained only as needed to address the inquiry and any applicable obligations. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. You can request deletion of your correspondence by contacting the address below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

## Children

Perfect Fit is intended for players aged 13 and older. Parents and guardians should review the advertising and privacy-choice information above, including Google's linked policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact the address below so the matter can be addressed. Requests concerning information held by Google or an advertising partner should also be directed to that service using its privacy controls.
