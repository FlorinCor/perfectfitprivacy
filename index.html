Perfect Fit Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 October 2026

This policy describes how Perfect Fit (Android package com.florin.perfectfit), developed by Cordunianu Florin, handles information. It applies to version 0.1.0 and subsequent versions that use the same data practices.

Information handled by the game

Perfect Fit works offline and does not require an account. The game does not send personal information or gameplay data to the developer. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Data stored on your device

The game stores the following in its private application storage to provide its features:

  • Current level, completed levels, best move counts and completion times.
  • Hint usage and completed tutorials.
  • Unfinished puzzles, including object positions and puzzle state.
  • Preferences such as sound, vibration, reduced motion, and hint settings.

These records are used to resume puzzles, remember progress, and apply your preferences. The developer does not receive or sell these records, and the game does not share them with advertising or analytics services.

Android backup and device transfer

Perfect Fit allows Android to back up its saved progress and preferences and transfer them to another device. Depending on your device, account, and backup settings, your operating system or backup provider may store a copy outside your device. This is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by a server operated by the developer. The developer cannot access these backups. You can manage backup and restore through your device and account settings. For Google services, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local game data remains in the application's private storage until you clear it or uninstall the game. You can delete local records through Android Settings by opening Perfect Fit's app information and choosing the option to clear storage or app data. This removes saved progress and settings. Uninstalling also removes local app data; backup copies may remain under your backup provider's retention rules and may be restored when you reinstall. Manage or delete those copies through your backup provider.

Android's application storage protections help restrict access to local game data. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include will be handled to respond to your inquiry. This correspondence is processed through the developer's email provider and retained only as needed to address the inquiry and any applicable obligations. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. You can request deletion of your correspondence by contacting the address below, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its local storage and backup behavior are the same for all players. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact the address below so the matter can be addressed.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. The page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, or embedded third-party content. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when serving the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for GitHub's practices. Visiting this website is separate from playing the offline game.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated and its effective date revised. Any required notice or consent will be provided for changes that require it.
